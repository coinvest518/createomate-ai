# FDWA Marketing AI Agent 🚀

**Futuristic Digital Wealth Agency - AI Marketing Video Creator**
Website: https://fdwa.site

## Overview

This AI agent creates professional marketing videos for your AI consulting and development business using:
- **Creatomate** for video generation with your brand colors/styling
- **YouTube integration** via Composio for automatic uploads  
- **Pre-built templates** for consistent professional branding
- **No-code operation** - just natural language commands

## What It Does

### 🎬 Video Creation
- Creates branded marketing videos with FDWA styling
- Professional color schemes (Blue #0066FF, Teal #00FFAA)
- Multiple video types: Service promos, company intros, AI demos
- Automatic text, branding, and call-to-action integration

### 📤 YouTube Management  
- Direct upload to YouTube with professional metadata
- Auto-generated descriptions with FDWA branding
- Proper tags and categorization
- SEO-optimized titles and content

### 💡 Marketing Intelligence
- Pre-built content templates for AI consulting services
- Target audience optimization (business owners, startups, enterprises)
- Professional marketing copy generation
- Strategic video ideas and campaigns
